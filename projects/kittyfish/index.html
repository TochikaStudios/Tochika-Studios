Game Information

Help a little kitten to catch fishes while avoiding some thieves mice. Mobile game, with simple controls and a fun gameplay within 3 different levels.

You control a little kitten, that needs to catch fishes before the mice do. She is very hungry, but the mice don't care. Beware the gray mice, that move around chasing the kitten. The brown mice, they chase you only in the horizontal, trying to get you in the middle of the passages. And finally, the black mice, that tries to block your way vertically. After you catch all the fishes, you can go to the next level.

The game has 3 different levels, each one harder than the previous one. Challenge yourself and try to beat it without losing!

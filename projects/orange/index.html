Game Information

At night, an orange child hears strange sounds coming from their neighbors. Their fear is so great that monsters emerge from it, and to discover the truth, the child will have to face them. By contrast, during the day, the child suffers at school, where the monsters are the people themselves.

Orange is a game that tells the story of an orange child who ends up going through a difficult time in her life—a terrible time, a moment that changed her destiny forever. At night, the child begins to hear strange sounds that fill her with dread. He goes out looking for his mother in search of comfort, but on the way he ends up encountering a strange creature. Thus the story continues, alternating between day and night. During the day, the child goes to school. There, he ends up being bullied, making it a place of dread for him. One night, his parents go out and leave him alone at home. The child is afraid, but his parents are no longer there to help him. The child then decides to leave his room to figure out what was happening and, at the same time, face his fears.

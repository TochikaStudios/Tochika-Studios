About Us

Who are we?


Tochika Studios is a small Brazilian independent studio focused on game development. It was founded in 2022 by Guilherme Justino, with the primary goal of telling stories through games and comics. The studio has not yet been involved in major commercial projects, but is working toward its first one. It has primarily worked on small projects and game jams. It is currently working on an indie game called Orange.

With a small team of young artists and developers, all of whom are inspired and driven by the desire and determination to create something greater.
